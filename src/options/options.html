What Tuck knows

Read from your memory after an import, so Tuck knows what you work on and what is running out. Check it: a deadline read wrongly makes Tuck confidently wrong.

Page reading

Right before Tuck would nudge you about a page, it checks whether you are typing there (it notes when you last pressed a key, never which key), and reads up to 1,000 characters near where you type or from the middle of the window, so a work chat on a social site does not count as drifting. Secrets are removed first. On Meta it goes to Muse Spark 1.3, not Contributor, so it is not used for training. Only the verdict is kept, never the text. Pages with a password field and the sites below are never read.

Event log

Everything the companion noticed and decided: pages (URL and title), judgments, states, nudges and your answers. Page text is never kept. Kept for 7 days on this device.
